Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

GeoGallery is a private, invite-only photo and video gallery with a map and a location-guessing game. It is run by a private individual for friends and family, not as a business. It shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking, and does not sell, rent or use your data for marketing. Your data is used only to run the service for you.

Because GeoGallery stores your photos, videos and their locations, this policy explains exactly what is stored, who can see it, which service providers are involved, and how to get it deleted.

2. What data is processed, and why

2.1 Your account

You sign in through an identity provider (see section 4). When you sign in, for example with your Google account, GeoGallery receives and stores:

  • a technical identifier of your account at the identity provider (no password; GeoGallery never sees one),
  • your email address and display name,
  • your role (user or administrator), the time of your last sign-in, and whether your account is disabled.

GeoGallery does not request access to your Google contacts, Google Photos, Drive, calendar or any other Google data. It uses your email address and name only to identify you, to show your name to people you share albums with, and to let album owners find you when adding members.

Accounts are created only through an invitation. Invitations are stored with their role, creator, expiry and the account that used them.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the service you signed up for).

2.2 Sign-in sessions and API tokens

After you sign in, your browser keeps a single session cookie. It is technically necessary to keep you signed in, is not used for tracking, and expires after 30 days at the latest or when you sign out. The server stores only a cryptographic hash of it, together with its expiry and a sign-in token from the identity provider that is used only to sign you out there as well.

If you create personal API tokens in Settings, the server stores their name, a hash (never the token itself), a short hint of its beginning, their expiry and when they were last used.

GeoGallery sets no other cookies and stores nothing else in your browser.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; for the cookie, § 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (strictly necessary, no consent required).

2.3 Your photos and videos

When you upload media, GeoGallery stores:

  • the file itself, unchanged, including any metadata embedded in it (EXIF);
  • metadata read from the file: the date and time it was taken, the camera's GPS position if present, the camera make and model, the image or video dimensions, the video duration, the media type (photo, video, 360°) and the original file name;
  • how the capture time was derived: which time was used, the camera's clock as written, the time zone information in the file, the photo's GPS timestamp, the video's container time and encoding, and the file's modified time;
  • the file's last-modified time sent by your browser, used when the file has no capture date;
  • a fingerprint of the file, used only to skip duplicate uploads;
  • settings you make, such as a manually set location, 360° view direction or video keyframes.

Photos and videos can contain information about other people, such as their faces. Please only upload media you are entitled to share. GeoGallery does not perform face recognition or any other image analysis.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2.4 Location data

Location is central to GeoGallery: it places your media on a map and uses them in the guessing game. Location comes from three sources:

  • GPS coordinates embedded in your photos and videos by your camera or phone;
  • locations you set by hand;
  • GPS tracks you choose to import yourself (for example an export of your location history), which may contain detailed movement data with timestamps, altitude, accuracy and activity type.

GeoGallery uses imported tracks only to estimate where your own photos without GPS were taken, by matching their capture time against your track. Your tracks are also shown to you, and only you, to explain an estimate. They are never shown to other users, never matched against other users' media, and never combined with anyone else's data. GeoGallery also groups your media into trips by date; trips are visible only to you.

GeoGallery does not access your device's location. It only processes locations contained in files you upload or data you import.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

2.5 Albums and sharing

By default, everything you upload is visible only to you. If you put an item into an album, every member of that album can view it, including its location, capture time, original file name and the metadata embedded in the file. Album members see each other's names and email addresses. When you leave an album or are removed from it, the items you contributed are removed from it as well.

The administrator sees the list of users (name, email, role, status), but does not browse users' media through the application.

2.6 The guessing game

Your guesses are scored immediately and are not stored.

2.7 Server logs

When you use GeoGallery, the web server records technical access logs: your IP address, the time, the requested path, the response status and your browser's user agent. Sign-in requests are not logged. These logs are used only to keep the service running and secure, and are deleted after 14 days at the latest.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating a secure and working service).

3. Who can see your data

  • You.
  • Members of albums into which you put items (only those items, see 2.5).
  • The operator, who has technical access to the server, database and file storage and accesses them only to run, maintain and repair the service, or when you ask for it.
  • The service providers listed in section 4, only as far as necessary to provide their service.

Your data is not sold, rented, shared for marketing purposes or used to train AI models. It is disclosed to authorities only where the law requires it.

5. How long data is kept

  • Media, metadata, locations, GPS tracks, albums: until you delete them or your account is deleted. Deleting a photo or video in GeoGallery removes the file and its metadata, and removes it from every album.
  • Sessions: until you sign out, at the latest 30 days.
  • API tokens: until they expire or you revoke them (at most 365 days).
  • Server logs: at most 14 days.
  • Your account: until you ask for it to be deleted.
  • Backups: the database is backed up regularly; deleted data disappears from backups after at most 30 days.

To delete your whole account and all your data, email the address in section 1. Your account, media and everything connected to them will be deleted within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data stored about you (Art. 15),
  • have incorrect data corrected (Art. 16),
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17),
  • restrict processing (Art. 18),
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20), including your original files,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21).

To exercise them, email the address in section 1. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one where you live.

7. Security

All connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Files are kept in private storage and are only delivered through links that expire after 15 minutes. Session cookies and API tokens are stored only as hashes. Access to the server is restricted to the operator.

8. Children

GeoGallery is not intended for children under 16 without the consent of a parent or guardian.
